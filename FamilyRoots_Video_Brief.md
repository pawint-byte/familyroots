# FamilyRoots - AI Video Creator Brief

**Website:** familyroots.family
**Tagline:** Your people. Your story. One place.

---

## What Is FamilyRoots?

FamilyRoots is a platform that lets you build visual maps of the people who matter to you — and then do something real with them.

It's not just a family tree app. It works for any group: your family, your basketball team, your church, your school class, your friend circle, your fraternity. You add your people, define how they're connected, and FamilyRoots turns it into a living, interactive visualization you can share, grow, and even print on real products.

---

## Who Is It For?

- **Families** who want to map their roots, preserve stories, and stay connected across generations
- **Coaches and team managers** who want a visual roster they can share with players and parents
- **Teachers** who want to map their classroom, track groups, and share with families
- **Church leaders** who want to visualize their congregation and its ministries
- **Friend groups** who want a fun way to see their circle and invite new people in
- **Fraternities and sororities** mapping their lineage and chapter connections
- **Anyone** who has a group of people and wants to see them all in one place

## Key Features to Highlight in the Video

### 1. Build Your Tree in Minutes
Show someone creating a new tree — picking a type (family, sports team, school class), adding members with names and photos, and watching the visualization build itself automatically. Members appear as circles with photos, connected by lines showing their relationships.

### 2. It's Not Just Family Trees
Show the different tree types side by side: a family tree with parent/child relationships, a basketball team with coach/player connections, a church with pastor/congregation links, a school class with teacher/student roles. Each type has its own relationship vocabulary and visual style.

### 3. Share With a QR Code
Show someone generating a QR code for their tree. Another person scans it with their phone camera and instantly gets invited to join the group. It's the fastest way to grow your circle — hand out stickers, put it on a poster, or text it to someone.

### 4. Print Your People on Real Products
This is the big differentiator. Show someone taking their family tree visualization and putting it on:
- A **coffee mug** they drink from every morning
- A **poster** they frame and hang on the wall
- A **t-shirt** they wear to the family reunion
- A **throw blanket** draped on the couch
- A **tote bag** they carry everywhere
- **Stickers** with QR codes they hand out

Show the customization flow: pick your tree, pick your product, add a QR code so people can scan to join, add your school logo, preview it, and order — shipped right to your door.

Bulk ordering: a coach orders 30 mugs for the whole team. A teacher orders posters for every classroom. A family reunion organizer orders shirts for everyone. 10% discount kicks in automatically at 3+ items.

### 5. Connect Across Trees
Show two people who each have their own trees — say a husband and wife — connecting their trees together. Now they can see a merged view of both families in one visualization. They can selectively import members from each other's trees.

### 6. Preserve Memories with Memory Lane
Show someone adding a story to their tree — "The time Grandpa caught the biggest fish at the lake" — with a photo attached. It becomes part of the tree's permanent story feed. Voice notes too — record Grandma telling her favorite recipe in her own voice.

### 7. Import from FamilySearch
For the genealogy enthusiasts: show someone connecting to FamilySearch and importing ancestors going back generations. The system handles conflicts intelligently — if a name doesn't match, it asks you what to do instead of guessing.

### 8. AI Assistant
Show the built-in AI chat answering questions about your tree: "Who is my second cousin once removed?" or "How is Sarah related to Michael?" The AI understands your tree structure and gives real answers.

### 9. Privacy You Control
Show the privacy settings: who can see which members, three-tier visibility (public, extended, private), per-member overrides. Your tree, your rules. There's even a "Deadman Switch" — if something happens to you, your tree gets passed to someone you trust.

## The Emotional Hook

FamilyRoots isn't about data. It's about the feeling of seeing all your people in one place — your grandmother next to your grandson, your coach next to your teammate, your pastor next to your choir director.

It's about the 8-year-old who sees her family tree on a mug and points to every face: "That's Auntie! That's Pop-Pop!" It's about the coach who hangs a team poster in the gym and every kid who walks by sees their name and photo up there. It's about the reunion organizer who hands out t-shirts and suddenly everyone is wearing the same family on their chest.

Your people deserve more than a screen. FamilyRoots puts them in your hands.

## Suggested Video Structure (60-90 seconds)

**Opening (0-10s):**
"Who are your people?" Quick montage: family dinner, basketball practice, church service, school classroom, friend group laughing.

**Problem (10-20s):**
"Your family photos are scattered across phones. Your team roster is a spreadsheet. Your church directory is a PDF nobody reads."

**Solution (20-40s):**
"FamilyRoots turns your people into something you can see, share, and hold." Show the app: building a tree, watching it come alive with faces and connections. Quick cuts between different tree types.

**The Wow Moment (40-55s):**
"And then... put them on something real." Show the merchandise flow: tree on a mug, poster on a wall, shirt at a reunion, blanket on a couch. Someone scanning a QR code on a product and joining the group.

"Order 30 mugs for your team. Print posters for every classroom. Get shirts for the whole family reunion." Show the cart filling up, the bulk discount appearing.

**Closing (70-90s):**
"Your people. Your story. One place. FamilyRoots." Show the URL: familyroots.family. Final shot: someone holding a mug with their family tree on it, smiling.

## Visual Style Notes

- Clean, modern interface — the app itself is visually polished (dark/light mode, smooth animations)
- Warm, personal tone — this is about people, not technology
- Show real diversity in the groups: multi-generational families, youth sports teams, school classes, church groups, friend circles
- The product mockups (mugs, posters, shirts) should feel tangible and real
- QR code scanning moments should feel magical and instant
- The tree visualizations are the visual centerpiece — circles with photos connected by lines, the whole group visible at once
