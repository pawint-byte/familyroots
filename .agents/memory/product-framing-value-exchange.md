Andrew wants to help people improve their lives, but not for free. Paid software in exchange for real value. Always weigh “what’s in it for me” from BOTH sides: (1) founder/sustainability, (2) the member deciding whether to pay. Not charity, not scam — honest value exchange. FAQ and pricing should make that exchange clear. Apply on copy, features, and promo forever.

## Kinkeeper positioning

FamilyRoots is built for kinkeepers: the relative who keeps the family's photos, history, birthdays, and contact list up to date. Emphasize inviting relatives to claim their own profiles and record their own stories in a private, invite-only tree, so it is not all on the kinkeeper.

**Why:** The user explicitly requested this positioning for FamilyRoots.

**How to apply:** Keep future kinkeeper messaging focused on shared family participation, not making the keeper responsible for everyone's content.
