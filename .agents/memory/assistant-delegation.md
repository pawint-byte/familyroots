name: Member-directed assistant access
description: Product intent for delegated access by a member's own AI.
---

The intent is to get the member and the member's AI to interact with the site through permissions to handle tasks approved or directed by the member.

This capability adds no hosted model or chatbot, and the assistant does not use password login. It uses revocable member-granted access. Direct permitted calls run; newly added people remain drafts until accepted on the site. Do not invent relatives or guess ambiguous identities.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified that this is the member's own AI performing member-approved or member-directed tasks, not a site-operated autonomous assistant.

**How to apply:** Design delegated task execution within the member's existing access rights. Keep the member in control of grants, review, confirmation, and revocation; do not reinterpret the feature as independent goal-setting by FamilyRoots.
