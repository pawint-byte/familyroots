That's a really interesting concept. You're essentially describing peer-to-peer discovery — like how your phone finds nearby Bluetooth devices or Wi-Fi networks, but instead of networks, you're finding other PayGate members. A private channel that only members are "tuned into."

Here's the honest breakdown of what's possible:

What You Already Have (Pretty Close)

The Nearby Map + Push Notifications already creates a version of this. When a member goes live, nearby members get pinged. It's like broadcasting on a channel — only PayGate members are listening. The difference is it runs through the server, not directly phone-to-phone.

The WiFi/Bluetooth Approach (Native App Territory)

True device-to-device scanning — where your phone detects another PayGate phone nearby without the internet — uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) beacons. This is how Apple's AirDrop and Find My work. Each member's phone would:

Broadcast a tiny PayGate-specific signal (like a WiFi name, but invisible to non-members)
Other members' phones scan for that signal in the background
When detected, you get a notification: "A PayGate member is nearby"
Works in subways, at concerts, anywhere — no internet needed
The catch: this requires a native mobile app (iOS/Android). Browsers can't access Bluetooth scanning in the background. Your PWA can't do this today.

What We CAN Build Right Now

A "Members-Only Radar" feature that works within the PWA and feels like scanning:

Members opt into a "Radar" mode — they're broadcasting on the PayGate channel
Other members with Radar on get real-time proximity alerts: "2 members within 1 mile"
It uses location polling (every 30 seconds) instead of Bluetooth, but the experience is similar
Only members see members — it's a closed network
Private users can listen on the channel without broadcasting (Watch Mode already supports this)
A visual "radar sweep" animation on the map showing the scan happening
Think of it as upgrading the Nearby Map from "check who's around" to "your phone is constantly scanning for members near you and tapping your shoulder."

The Big Differentiator

This would make PayGate feel fundamentally different from every other dating app. No other app has this "members-only frequency" concept. It's like a private club where your phone is the membership card.
