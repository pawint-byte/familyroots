# Replit brief — Video/voice discoverability on the family tree

## Goal
Members with video or voice memories are invisible from the tree view — the only
way to know is to open each member's card. Make attachments discoverable and
addable directly from the tree.

## What to do

### 1. Attachment badges on tree member cards
- On each member card in the tree view, show small badge icons when that member
  HAS attachments: a play icon with count (videos), a mic icon with count
  (voice notes). Only render when count > 0.
- Position: bottom corner of the card, not overlapping the name or connector
  lines (note: cards currently overlap each other — see bug list #3 — place
  badges clear of card edges).
- Tapping a badge opens that member's card scrolled to the Videos / Voice Notes
  section.

### 2. Quick-add from the tree card
- Add a hover/long-press (desktop/mobile) quick action on the member card:
  "Add video memory" and "Record voice note".
- This opens the member card directly at the Videos / Voice Notes section with
  the upload/recorder ready — same dialogs as today, no new upload logic.

### 3. Empty state on the member card
- When a member has no videos: show "No video memories yet" + one warm line
  ("Their stories deserve more than text.") above the upload button.
- Same for voice notes.

### 4. Button copy
- Rename "Upload Video (max 100MB)" → "Add a video memory" (keep the size limit
  as small subtext, not the label).

## Do NOT change
- Upload/record backend logic, storage, player, or the playback corruption fix
  (separate brief). This is UI/discoverability only.
- No changes to tree layout algorithm itself — badges must fit existing cards.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Tree cards for members with videos/voice notes show the correct badges
      and counts; members without show none.
- [ ] Tapping a badge opens the member card at the right section.
- [ ] Quick-add from a tree card opens the correct upload/recorder for that
      member.
- [ ] Empty states render on members with no videos/voice notes.
- [ ] No overlap with card names, photos, or connector lines at default zoom.
- [ ] No new type errors; checkpoint before and after.
