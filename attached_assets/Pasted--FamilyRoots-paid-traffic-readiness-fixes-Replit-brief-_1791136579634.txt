But the "Record Life Event" dialog only has Event Type, Date, Title, Location, Description — no attachment fields. The product does not deliver what the FAQ promises.

**What to do (preferred):** Add photo/video attachment support to life events, reusing the existing member-profile upload/storage pattern:

- Make timeline cards clickable to open the event detail with its attachments.

**Fallback (only if the above is too large for this pass):** Change the FAQ answer to describe what actually exists today (video via the member-profile VIDEOS section), so the site never promises what it doesn't do. Do not leave the false promise in place.
